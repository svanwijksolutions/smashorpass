Privacy statement

This website belongs to SmashorPass in Novalja. This page explains in plain words what the site does with your data.

Last updated: August 2026

What this website stores

The site keeps two things in your own browser: the language you picked and whether you closed the notice at the bottom of the screen. Both stay on your device, they are never sent to us or to anyone else, and you can wipe them at any moment through your browser settings.

No analytics, no advertising

There is no visitor tracking, no analytics software and no advertising cookies on this website. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not share data with advertisers.

No contact form

This website has no contact form and no online ordering system, so there is no way to leave personal details here. Ordering goes by phone or WhatsApp, or simply at the counter.

Links to other websites

The site links to Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Maps. As soon as you follow one of those links you are on someone else's website, where their own privacy rules apply.

Hosting

The website runs on GitHub Pages. Like almost every web server, the hosting party can log technical request data such as an IP address. We have no access to those logs and we do not use them.
